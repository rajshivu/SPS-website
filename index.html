What electrical consultancy actually covers

An electrical consultancy sits between the electrician on site and the architect or plant manager planning the build — translating a facility's power needs into a design that's safe, code-compliant and efficient to run for decades, not just to switch on. Here's where our team goes deep.

Power Distribution & Design

HT/LT distribution, transformer sizing, switchgear and panel design for industrial and commercial loads.

Industrial Automation

PLC/SCADA integration and control-panel engineering that reduces manual dependency on the shop floor.

Renewable & Solar Integration

Solar PV sizing, grid-tie design and hybrid systems that cut dependency on grid power over time.

Energy Audits & Efficiency

Power quality studies, load analysis and tariff optimization that show exactly where cost is leaking.

Safety & Compliance

Earthing, lightning protection and statutory code review so your facility passes inspection the first time.

Maintenance & Monitoring

Scheduled maintenance and remote monitoring that catches faults before they become downtime.

Common questions

A few things clients usually ask before a first consultation.

What does an electrical consultant actually do on a project?

We assess the site, design the electrical system to meet load and safety requirements, oversee installation and commissioning, and stay on for support once the system is live — so you have one point of contact for the whole lifecycle.

Do you work on existing buildings or only new construction?

Both. Energy audits, panel upgrades and safety retrofits are common on existing sites; full system design is more typical for new builds or major expansions.

How does sustainability fit into a standard electrical project?

Wherever it makes financial sense — solar integration, automation to cut idle load, and efficiency upgrades that pay for themselves through lower energy bills over a defined period.
